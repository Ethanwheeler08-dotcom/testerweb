Our process

From discovery to launch — typically 6–10 weeks for a marketing site, 8–14 for an ecommerce build.

01

Discovery

We map your customer journey, current site analytics, competitor sites, and what's working. Strategy first, design second.

02

Design

High-fidelity mockups in Figma. We design key templates rather than every page — and keep the system flexible.

03

Build

Clean code, sensible CMS structure, proper accessibility, fast loads. We test on real devices, not just simulators.

04

Launch & iterate

Soft launch, monitoring period, then full migration. After launch, we keep testing and optimising as data comes in.

Questions we get often

What does a website build cost?

Marketing sites typically start at $12k; ecommerce builds (Shopify) start at $18k and scale up depending on integration complexity, custom features and migration scope.

Do you use templates or build custom?

Custom design, custom code. We don't sell themes off the shelf. We're efficient about it — our designers and developers use a strong component system — but each site is built to your brand.

Will I be able to edit content myself?

Yes — we use Shopify, WordPress, Webflow or Sanity depending on the project. You'll get a walkthrough at handover and a Loom video library you can revisit any time.

Do you handle hosting?

For custom sites, we recommend Vercel, Netlify or Cloudflare Pages. For WordPress, we use Kinsta or WP Engine. We can set up hosting in your name so you always own it.

What about SEO?

Every site we build has technical SEO baked in — clean HTML, semantic markup, schema, fast loads, and a sensible URL structure. For ongoing keyword and content SEO, we partner with specialist agencies.

Got a build in mind?

A few lines about your business and what's on the brief. We'll come back with a starting scope and a ballpark cost.
