A Framer website
built to win enquiries

We designed and built a fast, credible Framer site for a Western Australian law firm, structured from the first scroll to turn visitors into qualified enquiries.

Client
A WA law firm
Industry
Legal / Professional services
Services

Website design & build

The challenge

The firm had a solid reputation and a strong referral pipeline, but a website that did them no favours, dated, slow on mobile, and built more like a brochure than a tool that actually generated work.

Prospective clients who landed on it couldn't quickly understand which practice areas the firm covered, what to do next, or why they should pick up the phone. The enquiry form was buried, the copy spoke to other lawyers rather than to clients, and nothing about the experience reflected the standing of the firm behind it.

The brief: a new site that looked the part, loaded fast, and was built around a single job, turning the right visitors into enquiries.

Our approach

We chose Framer for the build, fast to ship, easy for the firm to maintain, and capable of the polish the brand needed without a heavyweight CMS behind it.

  • Mapped the site around the firm's core practice areas, with a dedicated, conversion-focused page for each
  • Rewrote the copy to speak to clients and their problems, not to other lawyers, with a clear next step on every page
  • Designed enquiry CTAs into the natural reading flow rather than hiding them in a contact page
  • Built the whole site in Framer with responsive layouts, considered motion, and accessible, semantic structure
  • Tuned for performance and Core Web Vitals so the site loads fast on mobile, where most enquiries start
  • Wired up form tracking and analytics so the firm can see which pages and practice areas actually drive enquiries

What we shipped

End to end, design through to launch:

  • A full Framer website, designed and built from scratch
  • Dedicated practice-area pages, each with its own enquiry path
  • Client-first copywriting across every page
  • Mobile-first responsive design with considered, restrained motion
  • Enquiry forms with validation, spam protection and instant email routing
  • Analytics and form-conversion tracking, plus a short handover guide so the firm can update content themselves
